
# Custom Robo

## Where is the settings page?

A Template YAML has been provided
- Starting items will either be the Ray01 set (default), arandom part for each Robo category, or Can Parts that will need to be selected before the first battle
- You can choose to either keep the Chapters in order or Shuffle them

## What counts as a check for Custom Robo?

- First-time use of all parts (they'll be yellow in-game until you've won a battle using that part)
- Every battle is a check

## Which items can be in another player's world?
- Robo Parts (the game is currently not progression locked, so you can play start to finish... requiring you can win with the parts you have, of course)
- Rahu Evolution (Each part of Rahu is gained in a Progressive system within the AP World, so more evolution means your Rahu access gets stronger!)
  - Rahu I
  - Penumbra I Pod
  - Rahu I Gun
  - Rahu II
  - Penumbra II Pod
  - Rahu II Gun
  - Grand Cross Bomb
  - Ultimate Legs
  - Penumbra III Pod
  - Rahu III Gun
  - Rahu III
- Chapter Memories and Dad's Watch, which will all be required to reach each day (and more importantly the Outside World!)

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The AP World circumvents the Parts Generator, so you'll receive the parts directly in your inventory. For Memories, you'll need those before you can progress to the next day, or you'll continue to repeat the current one!

## Changes from the vanilla game

Aside from the Parts Generator, all other function is still standard to vanilla (currently). "A New Journey" is the only mode currently implemented.

## What is my "go-mode?"

Obtain all nine of the Chapter Memory items plus Dad's Watch to reach the Outside World

## How Do I Win?
To win Custom Robo, you must reach the end of the game by defeating the greatest threat to humanity: Rahu

When Rahu is defeated once and for all, you win!
